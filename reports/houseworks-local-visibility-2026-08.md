# HouseWorks Plumbing & Gas — Why we're not showing up, and how to fix it

**Local visibility report · August 2026**
Prepared by The SEO Room

---

## The question we set out to answer

The Local Visibility board shows HouseWorks absent from ten sizeable suburbs, several of them closer to us than to the businesses that are ranking. If distance is on our side and we're still not there, something else is holding us back.

This report identifies what, using live data from the Google Business Profile, the website, and the actual competitor set in each suburb.

---

## The short version

Google ranks local businesses on three things: **distance, relevance, prominence.**

| Factor | HouseWorks | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Distance | 0–6 km from every target suburb | Not the problem |
| Relevance | Correct category, 6 supporting categories, 60+ suburb pages, 155 photos, full service list | Largely done |
| Prominence | 122 reviews | **This is the problem** |

The market median is 132 reviews. To hold a top-3 position in these suburbs you need roughly 200. The business that owns three of our five target suburbs has 470.

We are not losing on proximity or on effort. We are losing on review volume, and it is the one signal we have barely worked on.

## Finding 1 — Relevance is in good shape

This is worth stating plainly because it is where most agencies would tell you to spend money, and it would be wasted.

| Signal | Status |
|---|---|
| Primary category | Plumber — correct |
| Additional categories | 6 (Drainage, Bathroom Renovator, Gas Installation, Hot Water Supplier, Water Filter Supplier) |
| Services listed | 57 |
| Photos | 155 |
| Business description | Complete, names the suburbs served |
| Reply rate to reviews | 95.1% |
| Suburb landing pages | 60+, including Canning Vale, Thornlie, Willetton, Como, Hamilton Hill, Spearwood |

For context on how strong this already is: of the market leaders we examined, **Hilton Plumbing (3,983 reviews), Baywood (470), Ausco (378) and General Plumbing Co (408) carry no additional categories at all.** Baywood has 11 photos to our 155.

Category breadth and photo count are not what's driving rankings in this market. We are ahead on both and still absent.

### Why we're invisible in our own backyard

HouseWorks' profile sits within 5 km of both Canning Vale and Thornlie. We were still absent from both result sets — because the tenth-placed business in Thornlie had 129 reviews and in Canning Vale 126. Both above us.

That is not a data glitch. It is the finding: **we are not in the top ten by prominence for the suburbs on our own doorstep.**

## Finding 3 — The threat is Baywood, not Hilton

Two different kinds of competitor:

| Tier | Who | Can we beat them? |
|---|---|---|
| Metro operators | Hilton (3,983), Plumbdog (1,253), G.X.R (672) | No — not on volume, not this year |
| **Cross-suburb mid-tier** | **Baywood (470)** | **This is the fight** |
| Single-suburb locals | 46–133 reviews | This is where we currently sit |

Baywood runs from a single Canning Vale base and ranks **#1 in Canning Vale, Thornlie and Willetton** — three of our five priority suburbs, and exactly the territory we need.

**Important correction to the board:** for Canning Vale specifically, Baywood is *closer* to the suburb than we are. The "beaten by businesses further away" flag is true of some competitors there, but not of the leader. Canning Vale is a genuine fight on their home ground.

## Finding 5 — Two fixable gaps outside reviews

### The service area list is full and misallocated

Google allows 20 service areas. We have used all 20. Four of our ten priority suburbs are missing from the list:

| Suburb | Population | Has landing page | In GBP service areas |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canning Vale | 33,059 | Yes | Yes |
| Thornlie | 23,153 | Yes | **No** |
| Willetton | 18,187 | Yes | Yes |
| Como | 14,138 | Yes | **No** |
| Hamilton Hill | 10,387 | Yes | **No** |
| Success | 10,148 | **No** | **No** |
| Spearwood | 10,034 | Yes | **No** |
| Piara Waters | 9,116 | **No** | **No** |
| Harrisdale | 9,076 | **No** | **No** |
| Atwell | 9,063 | **No** | **No** |

Because the list is full, adding a suburb means removing one. Current entries that are lower value than Thornlie (23,153 people) include Ferndale, Lynwood, North Lake and Shelley. Worth a deliberate swap rather than leaving 23,000 people uncovered.

### We are closed when plumbing emergencies happen

HouseWorks trades **Monday to Friday, 7am–4pm.** No Saturday. No Sunday.

Of the 50 competitor records we pulled:

| Their hours | Count |
|---|---|
| Open Saturday **and** Sunday | 19 |
| Open Saturday only | 15 |
| Weekday only, like us | 9 |

Thirty-four of fifty are open at least one weekend day. Every one of the top six by review count is open Saturday.

Two consequences: Google is less likely to surface a closed business for urgent queries, and every weekend burst pipe in our service area goes to someone else — along with the review that follows it.
